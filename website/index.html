A clipboard with a memory

  • Every copy, kept

    Text, links, images, files and colours are saved the moment you copy them, and kept until you say otherwise.

  • Find it by typing

    Search everything you've copied and narrow it down by app, type, date or pinboard.

  • Pinboards for what you reuse

    Colour-coded boards for the replies, links, colours and commands you paste every week.

  • In step on every Mac

    Folder sync keeps your Macs together, encrypted before anything leaves this one.

Your clipboard,
on call for your tools

A built-in MCP server lets the editors, terminals and agents on your Mac search what you've copied, read it and save new items. It listens on this Mac only, and nothing connects until you approve it.

Search Super Paste for the error log I copied this morning and open an issue with it.

Read the MCP guide

Private by default

Your history stays in a database on your Mac. No account, no analytics, no servers. Password managers are never recorded, and you choose what else to ignore.

Made for the way you work

Copy a command, then another, and paste them in order with the Paste Stack. Your snippets stay one search away.

Every colour you pick turns into a card with its hex code, ready to paste into the next file.

Keep replies, sign-offs and quotes on a pinboard and drop them in with a keystroke.

Open a search, a pinboard or Settings from Shortcuts, Raycast or Alfred with a superpaste:// link.
